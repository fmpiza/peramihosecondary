Electronic Resources

Central hub connecting several electronic components

In modern education, electronic resources act as the digital infrastructure that integrates into a School Information System (SIS) to manage administrative tasks, student records, and day-to-day operations

  1. 01

    Infrastructure & & Network Resources

    Secure cloud hosting servers, and hardware like school tablets and desktop workstations—ensures the SIS stays online and accessible.

  2. 02

    Learning Management Systems (LMS)

    Manage course delivery and assignments. When integrated with an SIS, class lists and course registrations sync automatically from the SIS into the LMS

  3. 03

    Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Modules

    Broader financial and HR software components manage backend processes like staff payroll, vendor procurement, and school budgeting

  4. 04

    Communication & Safety Systems

    Automated SMS gateways, integrated email servers, and campus visitor security systems sync with the SIS database to look up emergency parent contact information in real-time
